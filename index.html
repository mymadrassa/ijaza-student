Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before enrolling

Do I need any experience to start? ▼
No experience is needed at all. We welcome complete beginners of all ages. Your tutor will assess your level in the first session and build a plan just for you.
What platform are lessons held on? ▼
Lessons are held via Zoom or Google Meet. Both are free to download and easy to use on any device — phone, tablet or computer.
Can I change my plan later? ▼
Yes absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Just contact us and we will arrange it from your next billing cycle.
Is there a free trial? ▼
Yes — we offer a free introductory session so you can meet your tutor and experience the lesson before committing to a plan.
What if I need to cancel a session? ▼
No problem. We ask for at least 24 hours notice to reschedule. We understand life gets busy and we are always flexible.
