About the Programme

What is an Ijazah?

The Ijazah (إجازة) is a formal certification of Qur'anic recitation, granted through an unbroken chain of transmission — the Isnad — connecting the student directly back to the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

It is the highest scholarly honour a student of the Qur'an can achieve. Our programme combines traditional one-to-one recitation (Talaqqi) with the study of Al-Jazariyyah, the foundational poem of Tajweed sciences authored by Imam Ibn al-Jazari.

Each session, you recite directly to your teacher. Errors are corrected in real time. Your chain of transmission is authenticated and your Ijazah is granted upon completion of the full Qur'an.
